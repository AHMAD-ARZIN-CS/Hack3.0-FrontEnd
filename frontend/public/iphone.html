Try East Bay Link as an iPhone user

This is the real app at iPhone size. Tap and scroll inside the phone. Everything is demo data, so nothing you do is saved for anyone else.

  1. 1. Start as the demo student, or create an account to see sign-up and onboarding.
  2. 2. Community: open a post, mark it helpful, add a comment.
  3. 3. Academic: add a course, open its notes and study guides.
  4. 4. Housing and Discover: filter rooms, find food help, browse the marketplace.
  5. 5. Profile: see contribution credit for helping others.

On your own iPhone: scan with the Camera app, then in Safari tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. It opens full screen like an app.

Open the app

Demo data resets when the page reloads. Sign-in is simulated in this browser and never stores passwords.
